# Reference-derived formats

These patterns were abstracted from the supplied channel references on 2026-08-14. They are editorial patterns, not assets to copy. Never reproduce another channel's footage, narration, music, logo, font treatment, exact captions, or shot sequence.

## Channel pattern analysis

### 제공된 30초 정보형 채널

- Strength: a fixed top promise makes each rapidly changing evidence shot readable even with the sound off.
- Retention device: result first, a new document/photo/clip every three to four seconds, then a concise conclusion followed only by the separate common CTA tail.
- Required implementation: all new retention formats use the same black two-line top headline, yellow emphasis, and lower outlined caption hierarchy.
- Failure mode to avoid: copying the reference channel's logo, exact wording, footage, font, music, subtitle treatment, or shot sequence.

### 신비한 건축사전

- Strength: a surprising mechanism is visible before the explanation is complete.
- Retention device: open with the impossible-looking result, then reveal how each part works.
- Best fit: `story-explainer`, usually with diagrams, 3D explanation, licensed footage, or clearly disclosed reconstruction.
- Failure mode to avoid: long background before the mechanism appears.

Reference: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/oC1E1lDiknw

### 슥삭쇼츠

- Strength: the comparison or contradiction is understood immediately.
- Retention device: show A versus B, withhold one decisive difference, then reveal it quickly.
- Best fit: `quick-reveal` with one clear answer and rapid visual changes.
- Failure mode to avoid: stacking unrelated facts after the answer has already landed.

Reference: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/6-1AGXFcKDQ

### 발목장이

- Strength: a persistent top promise lets the viewer follow several short facts without losing the subject.
- Retention device: each fact sharpens the same premise, followed by a reversal or concise punchline.
- Best fit: `fact-stack` with a visible sequential fact counter, one linked verified claim per proof beat, varied evidence, a middle interpretation change, and a documented payoff callback.
- Failure mode to avoid: treating a witty closing line as permission to overstate the facts.

Reference: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/_e1ce9zJ6os

### 힘쎈너구리

- Strength: current information is framed around what changed and why it matters now.
- Retention device: result first, proof next, consequence last.
- Best fit: `fact-stack`, supported by maps, documents, official clips, charts, or on-screen evidence.
- Failure mode to avoid: turning a changing forecast or preliminary figure into a settled result.

Reference: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Jr2VAsHR5LA

### 지식코리야

- Strength: an escalating sequence creates suspense while the viewer waits for a concrete explanation.
- Retention device: establish the unusual outcome, add constraints in stages, place a meaningful turn near the middle, and fully resolve the opening promise.
- Best fit: `story-explainer` with verified reconstruction and 8-20 semantic beats.
- Failure mode to avoid: artificial suspense that delays an answer without adding evidence.

Reference: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/llHaFq1B8VQ

## Format router

Choose the smallest format that can deliver a satisfying factual payoff.

| Story shape | Format | Target | Retention spine |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One reversal, comparison, or answer | `quick-reveal` | 12-35s, 4-9 scenes | Hook, contrast, reveal, payoff |
| Three or more linked facts, numbers, or a current update | `fact-stack` | 20-55s, 6-12 scenes | Result, numbered proof stack, middle turn, impact, callback payoff |
| Mechanism, reconstruction, or escalating event | `story-explainer` | 35-120s, 8-20 scenes | Outcome, context, evidence, turn, escalation, payoff |

New projects always use `quick-reveal`. Continuous-flow is the 12-35 second default; explicit visual-first requests use 8-14 narration-free seconds with generated no-vocal music. Record the evidence-based angle and confidence in `project.json.format_selection`, and narrow complex coverage to one answer that fits the selected delivery mode. The other format examples on this page are legacy guidance for existing projects only.

## Entertainment without distortion

- Create fun through surprise, contrast, visual proof, pacing, and a satisfying answer.
- End each beat with a forward-driving phrase or visual question that the next beat answers.
- Put a real turn or new proof near the middle; do not merely repeat the opening in louder words.
- Let humor come from harmless contrast or wording only. Do not use jokes for deaths, injuries, disasters, crime victims, minors, health emergencies, or other sensitive harm.
- If the story has no honest surprise or payoff, choose a clearer topic instead of forcing clickbait.
