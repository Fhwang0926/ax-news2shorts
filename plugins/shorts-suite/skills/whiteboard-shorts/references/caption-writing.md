# Viral punch caption writing

Write captions as a five-beat viewer reaction, not a formal explanation.

## Required beat shape

1. `hook`: point at the visible oddity with an unfinished question.
2. `setup`: name only what the frame reveals.
3. `rehook`: overturn the first assumption with `인 줄 알았는데`, `근데`, or an equivalent contrast.
4. `escalation`: describe the next visible action with one familiar internet metaphor.
5. `payoff`: start with `반전:` or an equally immediate reveal and state the visible truth.

For other scene counts, preserve hook, rehook, and payoff and merge adjacent beats.

## Writing constraints

- Use `viral-punch`, at most two lines, at most 36 Korean characters, and one idea per scene.
- Prefer spoken fragments over report language. Use a concrete noun or verb in every line.
- Make line 1 work without line 2. Use line 2 for the turn or punch.
- Allow obvious metaphors such as `레이더`, `새로고침`, or `프레임 멈춤`; do not state an unobserved emotion, diagnosis, job, or intention as fact.
- Do not reuse stale phrases such as `신원 확인 중`, `감시는 계속된다`, `경계 근무 완료`, or `결론: 장식이었습니다`.
- Do not add exclamation marks to every scene. Reserve punctuation emphasis for the reveal or payoff.

## Example transformation

```text
Before: 용의자 발견… 그런데 너무 안 움직인다
After:  고양이 발견!
        근데 프레임이 멈춤

Before: 결론: 장식이었습니다
After:  반전: 고양이 아니었음
        장식품한테 완전히 낚임
```
