# Rights and safety policy

Supported rights states are owned, licensed, permission_confirmed, public_domain, unknown, review_required, and not_permitted.

- not_permitted blocks initialization, acquisition, and render.
- unknown and review_required remain local-review-only.
- other states preserve supplied evidence but are not a legal or platform determination.

For unknown and review_required sources:

- use no more than eight seconds per source excerpt;
- use no more than eighteen source seconds in total;
- reject a composition that uses at least 90 percent of the source;
- reject simple translation and near-full reupload.

Acquisition uses only the selected canonical URL and local yt-dlp without cookies, login, playlist, CAPTCHA, DRM bypass, or download mirrors.

Keep watermarks. Do not blur, cover, crop away, or inpaint creator attribution. Source-caption blur is permitted only after visual review and only where it does not overlap watermarks, animal faces, or human faces. A Korean source-caption override must be time-bounded, visibly labeled `원문 번역` or `원문 의역`, reviewed safe, and excluded from observation evidence; it may not cover creator attribution or invent identity, motive, or outcome.

For welfare-sensitive material, use visible behavior language and verified facts. Do not diagnose emotion, motive, illness, abuse, treatment, or recovery from the video alone.
