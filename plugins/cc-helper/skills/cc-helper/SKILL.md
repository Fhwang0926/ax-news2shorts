# cc_helper

Create a source-researched local editing draft with deliberately conversational commentary. The human editor, not this skill, finishes narration, timing, sound placement, rendering, and publication.

## Start

Run the local preflight before research or project work:

```text
python3 <plugin-root>/scripts/cc_helper.py doctor --json
```

Read [workflow.md](references/workflow.md) before researching or scripting. Read [project-contract.md](references/project-contract.md) before writing project JSON, collecting files, or preparing a CapCut clone.

## Workflow

1. When no topic or URL is supplied, research broadly across entertainment, sports, broadcasting, SNS, communities, and everyday controversies from the last 48 hours. Present no more than three cross-checked candidates and stop for the user's Candidate ID. Do not create a project or collect assets before selection.
2. After selection, run `init` with the selected title, Candidate ID, category, and known canonical source URLs. Complete `research.json` with the selected candidate, clear `who`, `what_happened`, `why_people_react`, and source-linked facts.
3. Write one curiosity-gap title split into a white first line and yellow second line, each at most 16 Korean characters. Build one chronological message as 7–10 short narration beats in casual Korean that sounds like explaining the issue to a friend. New projects use `narration_flow_mode: conversational-chain` and explicit `flow_role` values in this order: `hook → setup → trigger → explanation → reaction → response → consequence → resolution → aftermath`; the same role may repeat, but the story may not jump backward. Do not use formal `합니다` or `했습니다` narration endings. Vary transitions such as `했다는데`, `였고`, `이었음`, `말했지만`, `이어졌고`, and `했다고 함`; never place two `~데/~는데` endings next to each other or finish the last two beats with `~함`. Confirmed factual wording is not required for narration: inferred intent, exaggeration, metaphor, and slang such as `손절`, `난리`, or `폭발` are allowed when they make the explanation more natural and punchy. Preserve a user-specified colloquial expression instead of weakening it merely because a source does not use the same wording. Keep exactly 15 structural scene slots, but use one stable visual per narration beat so internal clause-caption changes do not create unnecessary image cuts.
4. Prefer direct real material in this order: incident video or frame, official page or post, broadcast/SNS/community capture, relevant source photo, location or contextual B-roll, then a disclosed fallback. A concrete incident, announcement, cancellation, filing, or official-list change uses `visual_requirement: direct_incident`; never fabricate a CCTV frame, article, post, broadcast, or official-page capture. Original source text, attribution, and watermarks remain when they are genuine evidence. Community source URLs are automatically normalized with `community-capture-safe`; use `collect-assets --community-capture` when the community host is not in the built-in list. This mode keeps the whole capture inside the CapCut template-safe region and forbids portrait fill or edge cropping. For documents, articles, posts, or official pages whose words carry the evidence, use `compose-readable-source` to crop and enlarge only original source pixels into a 1000×720 card; do not retype, regenerate, highlight, or place the full wide page over a blurred background. After collecting and viewing both the source and normalized file, run `review-asset` with `--display-focus`, `--preview-checked`, and, for text evidence, `--evidence-readable --visual-anchor-term ...`. Review approval never changes `rights_status`.
5. New projects use `person_visual_mode: stylize-or-remove` and `public_figure_style_mode: obvious-editorial-eye-band`. If a selected screen visibly contains a public figure, create a one-hop, text-free `editorial-animation` derivative from a reviewed, non-synthetic `source_photo`, `official_evidence`, or `source_capture` while preserving identity, clothing, and factual context. The derivative must use an obviously editorial treatment at Shorts preview size and a visible horizontal `editorial-ruler-eye-band` whose ruler-like ticks remain visible; record both motif approvals during asset review. The eye-band is only an editorial motif: it is not anonymization, licence clearance, consent, or a defense against portrait/publicity-right claims, so the person remains recorded as identifiable and `rights_status` remains unchanged. If people are incidental to an article or official-page capture, crop them completely out while preserving the relevant original source text and attribution. Never synthesize a recognizable private person, minor, victim, or staff member; replace a people-dependent incident view with a reviewed, explicitly disclosed `non_identifying_fallback`, or crop the person out. Record `people_visible` and `people_treatment` during review. The original real asset remains in provenance even when the storyboard selects a derivative or crop.
6. Keep every collected asset at `rights_status: unreviewed` unless the user separately supplies evidence. This does not block a local CapCut draft, but it keeps `publish_blocked: true` and `status: local_review_only`. Preserve source URLs, visible attribution, and watermarks. Never bypass login, CAPTCHA, paywall, DRM, hotlink protection, or region controls.
7. New storyboards use `pacing_mode: narration-hold`, `narration_flow_mode: conversational-chain`, `caption_sync_mode: clause`, `visual_validation_mode: evidence-first`, `display_validation_mode: short-preview`, `final_visual_review_mode: player-check`, `person_visual_mode: stylize-or-remove`, `person_motion_mode: subtle-deterministic`, and `narration_performance_mode: reviewed-external`. New projects separately use `project.json.youtube_upload_mode: copy-handoff`, so upload-copy edits do not invalidate visual QC. Every scene records linked `fact_ids` plus one explicit `visual_requirement`: `direct_incident`, `direct_subject`, `contextual`, or `symbolic_allowed`. Only content- and quality-approved assets may reach CapCut; a source portrait remains inventory-only, direct claims reject symbolic synthetic images, and fallback use records a concrete reason. Normalization records its foreground box, ratios, and PNG hash. General screens require at least 55% foreground width, 28% height, and 22% area; `source_text` or `mixed` screens additionally require 35% height and 30% area. Allocate one to three consecutive structural slots to each narration beat. Every slot in the same beat keeps one stable asset, path, base geometry, material crop, position, and scale while the lower caption changes by spoken clause; a beat change selects the next visual. Each scene after scene 1 needs an opening `caption_anchor` that begins within the first two spoken words, and the caption itself must begin with that anchor and retain the narration clause's final connective or ending. The ordered scene narration for a beat must reconstruct the actual beat narration. Never use a caption that reveals the next clause or beat early. Put SFX only on the first slot of a beat. When an external Typecast WAV already exists, record timestamp or waveform cues in `handoff/narration-timing.json` and its per-beat emotion, tempo, planned pause, WAV-measured pause, audio analysis, file hashes, storyboard-narration hash, and review kind in `handoff/narration-performance.json`. Automated technical review may validate a local draft with a warning, but publication still requires a separate human listening approval. Match the storyboard end to the audio length within 0.15 seconds and do not pad to a fixed runtime. For every same-beat follower caption, select the first clear spoken-phrase onset rather than the beginning of a broad silence or low-level tail, and record `onset_policy: strong_speech`, `spoken_prefix`, `strong_speech_onset_seconds`, and `frame_rate: 30`; the selected scene start must be within one frame of that onset. Fill `project.json.youtube_upload` with copy-ready title, description, one to five hashtags, search tags, pinned comment, category, Korean language, audience, altered-content decision and reason, private visibility while blocked, matching thumbnail copy, and one to five representative source IDs. Do not create narration audio inside cc-helper. BGM is absent unless the user supplies a local file. Run `validate --stage assets` and fix errors before CapCut preparation.
8. Run `prepare-capcut --dry-run`, show the exact destination, draft name, source template hash, duration, scene mappings, warnings, `reuse_existing`, and `next_action`, then stop for explicit clone or retime approval. Preparation may write project-side handoff files but must not write to the CapCut draft root. Once a cloned destination exists, keep its folder as `active_destination_name`, ignore later `v2`/`v3` destination requests, and prepare the same draft for retiming.
9. Only for the first clone, after explicit approval, confirm CapCut is fully closed and run `clone-capcut --confirm`. Never overwrite the base `news2shorts` draft or an existing destination, and never edit the shared `root_meta_info.json`. When `reuse_existing: true`, `clone-capcut` is a no-op that returns the existing path and points to `retime-capcut`; it must not create a version-up folder.
10. When an existing clone needs any approved content, image, caption, or timing update, confirm CapCut is closed and run `retime-capcut --project-dir <path> --confirm-existing` against that same active destination. The command must back up the active draft files and cover images, preserve the existing narration audio IDs and shared `root_meta_info.json`, keep `.bak` recovery snapshots untouched, update the narration duration when the approved WAV changes, restore each beat leader's segment and material geometry from the read-only base template, copy both geometry levels to same-beat followers, refresh draft covers from scene 1, and back up then invalidate only the exact active timeline's prerender cache so the player cannot reuse an old image. With `person_motion_mode: subtle-deterministic`, apply a hash-stable slow zoom or horizontal pan only to selected `editorial_animation` person assets; source text, incident evidence, and non-identifying fallbacks stay static. Split-slot beats interpolate one motion across the whole beat so a follower starts exactly where its leader ends. Never derive canonical geometry from the already edited destination.
11. Open the cloned draft in CapCut and review one actual player screenshot at the midpoint of every narration beat. Confirm both white and yellow title lines, the expected lower caption, the correct main visual, readable source content where required, and no clipping, overlap, blank frame, or unintended blur. For public-figure editorial animations also confirm that the strong editorial treatment, horizontal eye-band, and ruler ticks are obvious at player size. For every motion beat capture its start and end; for every split motion beat capture the frame immediately before and after the slot boundary. Confirm slow movement, face-safe framing, title/caption clearance, exact continuity, and no black frame or visual jump. Record screenshot hashes and approvals in `handoff/final-visual-qc.json`; it must bind to the current visual timeline, storyboard, and capcut-map hashes. A later visual change makes the review stale. Run `validate --stage capcut` only after this player review. Return the draft path, local-review status, editable-title/caption boundary, SFX cue file, replace-with-video cue file, final visual QC path, and every validation warning. Also display the full copy-ready YouTube title, description, hashtags, tags, pinned comment, thumbnail copy, audience, altered-content choice, recommended visibility, and representative sources from `handoff/youtube-upload.md`, while clearly showing `publish_blocked` and never implying that the copy is cleared to publish. Do not render, upload, schedule, or publish.

## Boundaries

- Keep `plugins/news2shorts` and the `plugins/shorts-suite` Whiteboard compatibility role unchanged. The external CapCut `news2shorts` directory is a read-only clone source.
- Keep one active CapCut clone per cc-helper project. Do not create `-v2`, `-v3`, or other version-up draft folders after the first clone; update the active clone through the confirmed retime path and its automatic project-side backup.
- A ranking or downloaded public file is not permission to publish. The plugin records provenance but does not perform licence clearance.
- Video files are retained in the handoff package; v1 puts their representative frame into the image-only template and records the manual replacement cue.
- Do not add native audio tracks to the CapCut JSON. A user-added narration track may be preserved and used as a retiming reference, but `retime-capcut` must not replace or alter its material or segment IDs.
- cc-helper validates an externally generated and reviewed Typecast WAV; it does not call Typecast or synthesize TTS itself.
- A stylized portrait or editorial eye-band does not anonymize an identifiable person and does not clear copyright, portrait, publicity, privacy, consent, or publication rights.
